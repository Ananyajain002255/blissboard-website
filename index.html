A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Curious? We've got you.

A few things you might want to know before booking.

Do I need prior art experience?

Not at all! Our activities are designed to be enjoyable for beginners. Just bring your curiosity and enthusiasm.

How do I book an activity?

Choose an activity, check its listed prices and contact BlissBoard to confirm the date, location and availability. You can also use the relevant booking platform where available.

Can I organise a private event?

Yes! You can enquire about birthdays, college events, group workshops and customised celebrations.

Are District and BookMyShow prices different?

The supplied price list currently shows the same amount for both platforms for each activity. Always check the platform checkout page for the final payable amount.
