A FEW LITTLE DETAILS

Frequently asked questions.

How do I book an experience?

Choose an activity and tap Enquire on WhatsApp. The BlissBoard team can confirm availability, date, venue and booking instructions.

Are the prices the same on both platforms?

The price list supplied currently shows the same amount for District and BookMyShow for each activity. Please confirm the final payable amount before booking.

Can BlissBoard organise private events?

Yes. You can enquire about birthdays, dates, friends and family gatherings, college events, corporate events and custom experiences.

Are the event photos upcoming events?

No. The photos in the event highlights section are memories from previous events. Contact BlissBoard for current event dates.
